## Sources (no external topics added)

- **Activity Book, pages 14–15**: prepositions of place, question ordering, town/neighborhood scenes.
- **Activity Book, pages 22–23**: birthday vocabulary, word search, vocabulary-in-context.
- **Activity Book, pages 24–25**: comparisons (Vick/Kira, Simon/Ryan, Nina/Alexa) and reading.
- **Activity Book, pages 26–27**: adverbs of frequency and sentence unscrambling.

## Three design roles and mutual review
This is a **one-time assisted creation and cross-review** (not three autonomous AI agents working indefinitely in the website).

1. **Agent 1 — Vocabulary Captain**: designed a comprehensive independent exam; reviewed model 2 for vocabulary relevance and ambiguous distractors.
2. **Agent 2 — Grammar Detective**: designed a comprehensive independent exam; reviewed model 3 for grammar placement, word order, and unambiguous answer choices.
3. **Agent 3 — Final Exam Master**: designed a comprehensive independent exam; reviewed model 1 for coverage, source-page alignment, and child-friendly exam style.

Changes applied during review:
- Removed identical question prompts and retained **120 unique question stems**.
- Clarified a misleading stem in Model 3's first question.
- Ensured 4 distinct choices and exactly 1 keyed answer per question.
- Added page references and short explanatory feedback for every question.
- Used **balloon** from Activity Book spelling, noting that the study guide prints **Ballon**.

## User experience
- 3 independently selectable 40-MCQ tests (120 in all).
- A, B, C, D answers; options lock after choosing; solutions are hidden until final submission.
- Question navigator, answered-counter, elapsed-time counter, source-aware explanation.
- Topic-by-topic score and wrong-answer review.
- Best scores saved privately to localStorage in the current browser.
- No secret keys, third-party scripts, paid voices, or backend.
- Writing exercises are **multiple choice only**; they do not measure free handwriting.
