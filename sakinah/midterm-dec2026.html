🪞 Page 34 · Reflexive pronouns

Rule: Use a reflexive pronoun when the subject and object refer to the same person or thing.

I taught myself. · The robot repaired itself. · We enjoyed ourselves.

SubjectReflexiveExample
ImyselfI dressed myself.
you (one)yourselfYou can do it yourself.
hehimselfHe looked at himself.
sheherselfShe taught herself.
ititselfThe robot turned itself off.
weourselvesWe protected ourselves.
you (many)yourselvesHelp yourselves.
theythemselvesThey made it themselves.

Book examples include Cassini destroying itself and children shading themselves from the sun.

🔊 Listen & practise the spelling words

Both ea and ee can make a long /ee/ sound. Learn how each word is spelled: the sound alone does not tell you which letters to use.

🪐 Page 45 · Comparatives

more + adjective + than = a greater degree.

Space is more interesting than an empty room.

less + adjective + than = a smaller degree.

The Moon is less crowded than Earth.

📖 Practise understanding a passage

Each mock exam contains a different original reading passage with four questions. Read first, then look for who, what, where, why and the main idea.

🔎 Smart reading strategy

1. Read the title and predict the topic. 2. Read every sentence slowly. 3. Underline the important clue in your mind. 4. Choose the answer supported by the passage, not by guessing.

💌 An Email to a Friend About a Useful Technology Tool

Teacher's checklist: date · To · From · 8 sentences · a useful technology tool · a question at the end.

🌟 Example (8 sentences)

Date: 8 December 2026 To: Sara From: Sakinah Hi Sara, I want to tell you about my tablet. It is a very useful technology tool. I use it at home. I read stories on it. I watch lessons on it. It helps me learn English. I love my tablet because it is helpful. What technology tool do you use? Best wishes, Sakinah
