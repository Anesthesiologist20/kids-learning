📖 Emilia’s New Year’s Adventure

Page 40By Kim Milne

Emilia is sitting on the sofa. She’s looking out the window.

“I’m so bored!” she thinks. It’s her parents’ New Year’s Eve party.

“How’s second grade?” asks a neighbor.

“Any mysteries?” asks another.

Emilia loves detective games. She likes reading detective stories, too.

She wants to be a famous detective!

“Why don’t you try out your new binoculars?” asks Mom.

“What a great idea! I have them in my room,” Emilia answers. She gets her binoculars and goes outside. She’s very excited.
