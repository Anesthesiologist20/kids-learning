Lesson 1 · Vocabulary

🔎 Detective Word Adventure

Read each word, its meaning and an example. These are the six words in your study guide.

😐Bored

Feeling that there is nothing fun to do.

“I am bored. Let's play!”
🕵️Detective

A person who looks for clues and solves mysteries.

“The detective finds a clue.”
😱Scared

Feeling afraid.

“The boy is scared of the dark.”
💡Turn on

Make a device or light start working.

“Please turn on the light.”
🌙Turn off

Make a device or light stop working.

“Please turn off the TV.”
🥷Thief

A person who steals things.

“The thief took a bag.”

💡 Remember: Turn ON = start. Turn OFF = stop. A detective looks for a thief. Scared means afraid; bored means not interested.

Lesson 2 · Grammar

🧠 Who Does It? Who Gets It?

Subject pronouns tell us who does the action. Object pronouns tell us who receives the action.

🚀 SUBJECT · does the action

PronounExample
II play football.
YouYou read a book.
HeHe eats an apple.
SheShe draws a picture.
ItIt runs fast.
WeWe go to school.
TheyThey play together.

🎯 OBJECT · receives action

PronounExample
meMom helps me.
youI see you.
himI call him.
herI help her.
itI like it.
usThe teacher helps us.
themI play with them.

⭐ Easy trick: Use He / She / We / They before the action. Use him / her / us / them after the action or after words such as with. you and it look the same in both groups!

Lesson 3 · Writing

🍦 My Sweet Writing Lab

The guide asks you to copy a sentence correctly on English lines, then write two descriptive sentences using ice cream or cupcake.

I like ice cream because it is cold and sweet.
🍦 I like ice cream because it is cold and sweet.
🧁 I like a cupcake because it is soft and delicious.
🍦 I like ice cream because it is yummy.
🧁 I like a cupcake because it is small and sweet.

📝 Writing checklist: Start with capital I. Use I like + food + because + description. Write a full stop . at the end. Keep your words neat on the English lines.
